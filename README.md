# NJ Fiscal Impact Analysis — 38 Communities & Camden

> Price-adjusted fiscal impact and break-even analysis for residential development across 38 New Jersey communities — what a new home costs a municipality and its schools, and what it pays back in property tax revenue.

## What this is

Fiscal impact analysis answers the question every NJ town asks about new housing: does it pay for itself? These workbooks model, by housing type, the municipal and school costs generated by new residents (using demographic multipliers) against the property tax revenue the new units produce — and find the break-even point.

## Method, in brief

1. Demographic multipliers (household size, school-age and public-school children by housing type) — see the companion [Demographic Multipliers Database](https://github.com/umerfarooq7424/demographic-multipliers-database)
2. Per-capita / per-pupil cost allocation from municipal and school budgets
3. Revenue side: assessed / equalized property values and tax-rate composition
4. Break-even: the home value at which revenue covers municipal + school cost, by housing type and bedroom count, price-adjusted
